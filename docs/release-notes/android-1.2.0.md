Installs over 1.1.0 (same signing key). Signing certificate SHA-256: `86:74:DA:2B:CE:50:26:22:F0:A0:2E:7A:22:50:96:DF:FB:D0:A9:45:17:82:66:25:C9:6C:DD:AF:37:90:D2:85`.

Home, stations, listening history and lyrics need the server side from the next plugin release (after 0.2.0).

## What's new
- **Now playing:**
  - a slowly drifting, blurred cover behind it (still under *Remove animations*);
  - **Up next** under the controls: tap for the queue, swipe it away to remove it (with Undo);
  - a **quality badge** such as *FLAC 24/96 · Hi-Res*: tap it for the signal path.
- **Sleep timer** (the moon button):
  - 15–90 minutes, a custom length, or the end of this track, album or queue;
  - for this player, its sync group or every player;
  - this phone fades out first: 30 seconds by default, set in Settings.
- **Lyrics** from your files' tags, or the *Music & Artist Information* plugin on your server:
  - synced lyrics follow the music, and tapping a line jumps there;
  - the current line shows small on Now playing (switch it off in Settings).
- **Mini player gestures:**
  - swipe left or right to skip;
  - swipe up to open Now playing;
  - long-press for *Play on…*.

  Every gesture is also a TalkBack action.
- **A Home you can arrange:**
  - Recently played, Stations, New Music, Most played and On this day;
  - reorder, hide and add shelves, including pinned menus;
  - the same on every device.
- **Stations** that keep going:
  - Decade, Genre, Forgotten favourites, Fresh and Deep cuts;
  - or *Save as station* on any mix in a menu.
- **Listening history**, kept on your server for 13 months: view it, clear it or switch it off in Settings.
