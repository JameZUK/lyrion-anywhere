Lyrion Anywhere **0.3.0** brings the server side up to date with Android [1.10.0](https://github.com/JameZUK/lyrion-anywhere/releases/tag/android-v1.10.0) and adds a lot to the web app. Update from *Settings › Plugins* in LMS; LMS restarts to load it.

**Install or update:** add this repository in *Settings › Plugins › Additional repositories* (if you haven't already), then install or update **Lyrion Anywhere**:

```
https://github.com/JameZUK/lyrion-anywhere/releases/latest/download/repo.xml
```

## Highlights

**Playing through gaps in the signal**
- **Longer resume grace:** a phone can ask to keep its player in LMS for up to 10 minutes while the signal drops (`--max-resume-grace`, 600 s by default). Players that don't ask still get 60 s.
- **Listening history** counts what was really heard during a gap, judged by the player's own clock. However the reports arrive, a session is never credited more than the wall time it ran.
- **Replays and downloads:** streams can be fetched in part (`?limit=`), and Opus re-encodes are bit-identical every time, so phones can replay from their own cache and play Original FLAC streams from their downloads.

**Web app**
- **Now playing redesigned** for phones, with four tabs (Art, Lyrics, Info, Queue) and a new Glow.
- **Your listening** and **Your year in sound:** listening stats from your history.
- **Artists:** biographies, photos and album reviews.
- **Alarms** for any player you may control, and **ratings** (stars or a heart, per user).
- **Lyrics, the sleep timer, stations, listening history and a customisable Home.**
- **Library and queue:** more ways to browse, sort and edit; **search on plugins**.
- **Car mode, system media controls, haptics and smart filters.**
- **Fixed:** a queue edit while the next track was buffered could cut the current one short.

**Plugin**
- **Security:** the settings form needs the page's own token, even with LMS's CSRF protection off. The admin secret goes only to the gateway the plugin started. http:// Public URLs need an explicit opt-in.
- **Supervision:** gateways orphaned by an LMS crash are stopped safely, stopping never blocks LMS, restarts back off, and the log is rotated.

The full list is in the [changelog](https://github.com/JameZUK/lyrion-anywhere/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
