Lyrion Anywhere **0.6.3** fixes the scrub-then-skip on iPhone and iPad (issue #12) at its root: a FLAC seek from LMS arrives without a header, and the player's decoder could not take one. Two gateway faults and a Safari stall found on the way are fixed too. Everything else is as in [0.6.2](https://github.com/JameZUK/lyrion-anywhere/releases/tag/v0.6.2) and [0.6.0](https://github.com/JameZUK/lyrion-anywhere/releases/tag/v0.6.0), which added the built-in Cloudflare Tunnel. Android [1.15.5](https://github.com/JameZUK/lyrion-anywhere/releases/tag/android-v1.15.5) remains current. Update from *Settings › Plugins* in LMS; LMS restarts to load it. The web app updates itself in the background and offers a reload when it is ready.

## What was wrong (#12)

**The skip:** LMS serves a FLAC seek by jumping into the file, with no header in front (its stock `flc flc * *` rule is a plain pass-through, and FLAC is the one format LMS never builds an initial block for). Hardware players and squeezelite decode such bare frames; this player's decoder needed the `fLaC` header, so every scrub at Original ended in "Decoder does not support file format" and LMS moved to the next track. It did so on every stock LMS, 9.1 or 9.2; it went unreproduced for so long because the test server had a custom transcoding rule that re-encodes seeks through the flac binary, header and all. The player now makes the missing header from the first frame and plays on from the seek.

**Found on the way, on an iPad against LMS 9.1.1**, and fixed too:

1. **Safari stops delivering a response body whose consumer paused.** The web player's download worker paused reading whenever the player's 8 MiB input buffer was full. After every such pause Safari's `read()` never returned again, with megabytes already received and sitting unread.
2. **The gateway ended a quiet stream after 60 s.** With the worker no longer acknowledging, the gateway took the player for idle and ended the response, which a player on a long track hit as a matter of course.
3. **The resume was refused by a few KB.** The player resumed from the byte it had received, which could sit just short of the gateway's replay window: HTTP 410, the stream given up, the track reported as finished, and LMS moved to the next one.

## Player core

- **A FLAC stream that begins at a frame gets its header made** from the first frame header (rate, channels, bit depth; the previous stream's when the frame does not say), checked against the next frame's header so a sync pattern inside the audio is never mistaken for one. A frame the decoder cannot parse mid-stream is skipped rather than ending the track. The Connection log notes the made header. This applies to the web app and, with its next build, Android.

## Web app

- **The download is read as it arrives, whatever the player wants just now.** The worker keeps reading into its own queue and feeds the player from there, so Safari's stream is never held back. Only what the player has taken is acknowledged, so the gateway's replay window bounds the queue. As a safety net, a response that brings nothing for 15 s while the player is hungry is abandoned and resumed from the byte reached.
- **Hi-res audio no longer sits on the browser's media buffer quota.** An iPad gives an audio-only media buffer about 5.5 MB, and the element is fed uncompressed FLAC frames (576 KB/s at 24/96). The player now sizes how far ahead it feeds, and how much played audio it keeps behind, from the stream's data rate: CD quality is unchanged, 24/96 feeds about 4.5 s ahead and keeps 1.8 s behind, 24/192 sits on 3 s and 1 s floors. Before, every append on a hi-res track waited for the play head.
- **A play head that sits inside buffered audio without moving is kicked** (a seek to the same place, at most once per five seconds): on an iPad the first track of a session once stayed at 0.0 s with ten seconds buffered.
- **Connection log:** acknowledgements are noted (one line per five seconds), a stream fed but not acknowledged says so, a full player that holds the queue up is noted after ten seconds, and the media buffer lines now carry the play head, the buffered span and the segment size, rate-limited, instead of flooding the report several times a second on hi-res audio.

## Gateway

- **A stream's replay window always reaches back to the client's last acknowledgement**, so a resume from the byte received is never refused.
- **A slow or paused client is not an idle one:** the idle backstop is now ten minutes, and the relay logs a response that has waited more than five seconds for an acknowledgement, with its position, received and acknowledged bytes, and an acknowledgement it ignored.

## Mobile

- The ring buffer between the engine and the audio output could lose a timed start armed while paused (a race between the start frame and the hold), leaving the phone silent at a sync-group start. Fixed, with a regression test; it ships with the next Android build.
