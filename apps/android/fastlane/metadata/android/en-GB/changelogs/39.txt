The quality badge says what you hear: the weakest link first (Hi-Res, Lossless, Transcoded or Lossy), then the stream actually heard. Tap it for the signal path as a block diagram from file to phone, and change the streaming quality there. Fix output level at 100% (Settings › Sound) keeps the phone's volume the only one that matters. Over http:// the app needs the server's private IP address, and the message now says so. Needs plugin 0.7.3 or later (protocol 2).
